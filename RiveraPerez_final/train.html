Rats in NYC

Brown rats spread across Europe and the Americas after arriving on ships from Asia in the 1700s. They quickly outcompeted black rats and eventually became the dominant rat species in cities like New York.

Fun Fact: It's an urban myth that there are as many rats as people in New York. In fact, the real number of rats is closer to one-quarter of the number of people (or about 2 million rodents).
