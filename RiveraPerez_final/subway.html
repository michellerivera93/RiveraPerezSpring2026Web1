About the MetroCard

The MetroCard was New York City's swipe fare card for decades. It is now being replaced by OMNY, the MTA's contactless tap-and-go payment system.

The MTA announced that MetroCard sales and distribution would end on December 31, 2025. MetroCards will still be accepted into 2026, and the exact final acceptance date will be announced later.
