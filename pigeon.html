The NYC Pigeon

The pigeons seen around New York City are usually rock pigeons. Before they became common city birds, pigeons had a long history with humans. People domesticated pigeons for food, companionship, and message carrying.

Many city pigeons are descendants of domesticated pigeons that escaped or were released. They adapted well to cities because the structures in NYC are similar to the rocky cliffs their wild ancestors used for nesting.

Fun Fact: During World War II, homing pigeons were extensively used for communications by the Axis and Allied forces, particularly for sending messages back to their home bases.
