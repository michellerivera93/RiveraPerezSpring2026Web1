final project proposal

michelle rivera perez

theme
new york city adventure! the user will navigate the streets of new york city, interacting with different objects along the way
description- what will the contents of your website be
i would like to make my project resemble a video game and possibly take inspiration from the "different layers of the sea" project where the user nagivates through different layers/links. In this case, the user would navigate through the different "layers" of nyc, starting above ground and then going underground
what style of fonts will you look for?
there is a large contrast between different parts of nyc. To show this, I will probably include some kind of graffiti font and a "fancier" font on a different page
what colors are you considering and why?
to emphasize the constrast between different parts of nyc, i will use darker, cool toned colors for underground areas and brighter colors when the background is above ground.
what will your javascript element be?
drag and drop- since i want to make my project resemble an interactive game, i was thinking of using the drag and drop demo from our previous class to have the user dress up their character.
what will your p5 element be? Will it be animated? interactive?
sketch with music? i would like to include a page where the user enters a concert or club and would like to utilize the sound library from p5js
What will your jquery element be?
selector tool- whenever the user swipes their metrocard, the border will turn red
